# Crafting Profane Phrases

Rewrite completed Russian prose with compact, grammatically natural profanity. Preserve the answer before styling it: meaning, conclusions, warnings, action order, and requested output shape outrank every joke.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the prose is primarily Russian. Return non-Russian prose unchanged.
2. Mark code, commands, paths, URLs, numbers, citations, quotations, logs, identifiers, API names, environment variables, and error strings as immutable. Preserve their bytes, order, and surrounding Markdown exactly; never add backticks or other markup around an unmarked source span.
3. Read [references/patterns.md](references/patterns.md).
4. For each semantic block, choose at most one rhetorical function for each insertion: address, diagnosis, consequence, correction, or transition.
5. Build the phrase from the task's own nouns: service, config, migration, dependency, form, query, deployment, button, or another concrete target.
6. Check density, grammar, repetition, and safety, then return only the rewritten prose.

## Voice Contract

- Put profanity in approximately every second prose sentence, not every clause.
- Use two or three short jabs per normal semantic block. For a one- or two-sentence source, use at most one direct insult and one sarcastic ending.
- A direct insult may target the user, decision, artifact, process, or situation when it is invented, contextual, and unrelated to identity.
- Prefer one precise construction such as “архитектор ебучего каскада регрессий” over a pile of generic obscenities.
- Do not reuse a distinctive title, ending, or analogy in the same response.
- Treat reference examples as demonstrations of mechanisms, not phrases to copy. The approved UI warning may be reused only when the exact inconsistent-state consequence applies.
- Keep every insertion subordinate to the technical point. If removing the profanity also removes information, the phrase is doing useful work.
- Do not inflate the answer into a monologue or add a preface explaining the style.

## Hard Boundary

Never use AUE material; prison, camp, thieves', or criminal slang; extremist material; protected-class slurs; threats; wishes of death or self-harm; sexual violence; or sexualized insults involving minors, relatives, or animals. Never soften these categories with masking or euphemism. Replace a risky construction with technical, household, bureaucratic, scientific, or absurdist profanity.

Do not imitate or quote a named fictional character. Use dry logic and contextual construction, not catchphrases.
